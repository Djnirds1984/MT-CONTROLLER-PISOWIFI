Single-command bootstrap: sudo bash deploy/scripts/install-sbc.sh [/path/to/repo] installs packages, copies hotspot/, admin/, includes/, api/ into /var/www/aircoins, generates TLS cert and sodium key, initializes SQLite, prompts for first admin credentials, and enables ufw 80/443. Router configuration is applied via /import file-name=hotspot-external-portal.rsc after editing the site variables at the top of that script. Portal updates are synced with deploy/scripts/update-portal.sh without restarting services.
