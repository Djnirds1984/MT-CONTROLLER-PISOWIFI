`sudo ./install-sbc.sh [SOURCE_REPO_DIR]` provisions the full stack from the repo root (defaults to `../..` or `$AIRCOINS_SRC`); requires `hotspot/`, `admin/`, `includes/`, `api/` plus `deploy/lighttpd/aircoins.conf` and `deploy/php-fpm/aircoins-pool.conf` to exist. `sudo ./update-portal.sh [SOURCE_REPO_DIR]` re-syncs code without reinstalling. The MikroTik side is configured by editing the local variables at the top of `hotspot-external-portal.rsc` and running it in WinBox terminal or via `/import file-name=hotspot-external-portal.rsc`.
